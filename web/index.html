THE METHOD

Two kinds of evidence.
One reviewed count.

  1. Show a cap. Mark one to three examples. The same CountGD++ model detects bodies and caps in two separate passes.
  2. Connect the evidence. Geometry and one-to-one assignment link nearby caps to bodies. A linked cap adds no extra bottle.
  3. Resolve uncertainty. Unmatched caps and questionable bodies stay outside the count until you confirm or dismiss them.

Green boxes are automatic predictions, not verified inventory. The model can confuse cups and cans with bottles. Fully hidden objects cannot be recovered by this workflow.
